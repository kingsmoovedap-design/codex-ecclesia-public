☩ Codex Constitution

The foundational law of Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust

Preamble

We, the members of Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust, do hereby establish this Constitution as the supreme law governing the ecclesiastical organization, its trustees, heirs, and all sacred operations.

Article I: Establishment & Purpose

This organization is established as a 508(c)(1)(A) Ecclesiastical Organization dedicated to:

  • Preservation of sacred scrolls and records
  • Governance of ecclesiastical affairs
  • Administration of trusts and heirs
  • Management of Dynasty ecosystem
  • Advancement of lawful sovereign operations

Article II: Structure of Authority

The organization operates under a three-tier governance model:

  • Grand Architect: Supreme executive authority
  • Council of Trustees: Legislative and fiduciary oversight
  • Ministerial Assembly: Operational administration

Article III: Rights & Responsibilities

All members shall have the right to:

  • Access sacred scrolls and records
  • Participate in governance decisions
  • File documents and seek registrations
  • Receive fair and lawful treatment

Article IV: Treasury & Finance

The organization maintains a treasury governed by:

  • Fair market value principles
  • Transparent accounting practices
  • Sovereign coin (BSC) utility
  • Blockchain verification and audit trails

Article V: Amendments

This Constitution may be amended only by supermajority vote of the Council of Trustees, with notice given to all members.
