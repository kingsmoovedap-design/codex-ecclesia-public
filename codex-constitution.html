Preamble

We, the Living Souls of the Borders Dynasty, in sacred covenant with the Most High Creator, do hereby establish this Constitution for the Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust, a sovereign ecclesiastical organization operating under Divine Law and protected by 26 U.S.C. § 508(c)(1)(A).

In recognition of our unalienable rights endowed by our Creator, and in exercise of our inherent sovereignty as living men and women upon the land, we establish this sacred instrument to secure the blessings of liberty, prosperity, and divine protection for ourselves and our posterity.

Article I - Divine Authority

Section 1.1 - Source of Authority

All authority of this Ecclesia derives from the Most High Creator, the source of all Natural Law and unalienable rights. No earthly power supersedes Divine Law.

Section 1.2 - Legal Hierarchy

The hierarchy of law within the Ecclesia shall be:

  1. Divine Law - The Word of God and Natural Order
  2. Natural Law - Unalienable rights inherent to all living souls
  3. Covenant Law - Sacred agreements between parties
  4. Ecclesiastical Law - Internal governance of the Ecclesia
  5. Common Law - Custom, usage, and precedent

Section 1.3 - Ecclesiastical Exemption

The Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust operates as a 508(c)(1)(A) ecclesiastical organization, maintaining separation from state control in matters of faith, doctrine, and internal governance.

Article II - Sovereignty

Section 2.1 - Declaration of Sovereignty

The Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust is a sovereign ecclesiastical nation, existing in perpetuity under Divine Law. Its sovereignty is inherent, not granted by any external authority.

Section 2.2 - Territorial Jurisdiction

The Ecclesia exercises jurisdiction over all lands, properties, and assets held in trust, as well as spiritual jurisdiction over all naturalized members wherever they may be.

Section 2.3 - Recognition of Status

All members are recognized as living souls with full standing in Natural Law, distinct from any artificial legal persons or corporate entities.

Article III - Governance

Section 3.1 - The Crown

The Crown of the Ecclesia is vested in the founding lineage, serving as the supreme ecclesiastical authority and trustee of the sacred trust.

Section 3.2 - Council of Elders

A Council of Elders shall advise the Crown on matters of governance, doctrine, and dispute resolution. The Council shall consist of no fewer than three and no more than twelve ordained ministers.

Section 3.3 - Ministries

The following Ministries are established:

  • Ministry of Records - Maintenance of the Codex and all official documents
  • Ministry of Treasury - Financial stewardship and commerce
  • Ministry of Justice - Ecclesiastical courts and dispute resolution
  • Ministry of Diplomacy - External relations and treaties
  • Ministry of Education - Codex Academy and member training
  • Ministry of Stewardship - Property and asset management

Section 3.4 - Succession

Succession to the Crown shall follow the bloodline of the founding dynasty, with provisions for appointment in the absence of direct heirs.

Article IV - Treasury

Section 4.1 - Establishment

The Omega Treasury is established as the central repository of all Ecclesia assets, including but not limited to currency, property, and digital assets.

Section 4.2 - Borders Sovereign Coin

The Borders Sovereign Coin (BSC) is established as the official cryptocurrency of the Ecclesia, operating on blockchain technology for transparent and immutable record-keeping.

Section 4.3 - Treasury Operations

All treasury operations shall be conducted in accordance with the principles of ecclesiastical stewardship, with full accountability to the Crown and Council of Elders.

Section 4.4 - Tithing and Offerings

Members may contribute tithes and offerings according to their conscience, with all contributions protected under ecclesiastical exemption.

Article V - Membership

Section 5.1 - Naturalization

Living souls may become naturalized members of the Ecclesia through the sacred process of naturalization, including declaration of status and covenant agreement.

Section 5.2 - Rights of Members

All naturalized members shall enjoy:

  • Full standing in ecclesiastical courts
  • Protection under the laws of the Ecclesia
  • Access to ecclesiastical documents and services
  • Participation in governance as appropriate to their station
  • Issuance of ecclesiastical passport and identity documents

Section 5.3 - Duties of Members

Members shall conduct themselves in accordance with Divine Law, honor their covenant obligations, and support the mission of the Ecclesia.

Article VI - Ecclesiastical Courts

Section 6.1 - Jurisdiction

Ecclesiastical courts have jurisdiction over all disputes between members, matters of doctrine, and enforcement of ecclesiastical law.

Section 6.2 - Due Process

All parties before the court shall receive due process, including notice, opportunity to be heard, and the right to present evidence and witnesses.

Section 6.3 - Remedies

Courts may issue judgments, liens, orders, and other remedies consistent with ecclesiastical and common law principles.

Article VII - Commerce

Section 7.1 - Commercial Standing

The Ecclesia and its members operate in commerce under private ecclesiastical capacity, with full rights under the Uniform Commercial Code as adopted.

Section 7.2 - Fee Schedules

Official fee schedules shall be established for violations of members' rights, trespass upon ecclesiastical jurisdiction, and other commercial matters.

Section 7.3 - Liens and Security Interests

The Ecclesia may perfect security interests and file liens in accordance with applicable commercial law.

Article VIII - Amendments

Section 8.1 - Proposal

Amendments to this Constitution may be proposed by the Crown or by petition of two-thirds of the Council of Elders.

Section 8.2 - Ratification

Proposed amendments require approval by the Crown and three-fourths of the Council of Elders to be ratified.

Section 8.3 - Limitations

No amendment shall diminish the divine authority recognized in Article I or compromise the sovereignty declared in Article II.

Article IX - Diplomatic Relations

Section 9.1 - Recognition

The Ecclesia may seek and grant recognition to other sovereign entities, nations, and ecclesiastical organizations.

Section 9.2 - Treaties

The Crown, with advice of the Council, may enter into treaties and diplomatic agreements with other sovereigns.

Section 9.3 - Embassies

Ecclesiastical embassies may be established to facilitate diplomatic relations and provide services to members abroad.

Article X - Perpetuity

Section 10.1 - Eternal Establishment

The Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust is established in perpetuity, to endure for all generations until the end of time.

Section 10.2 - Inviolability

This Constitution and the Ecclesia it establishes are inviolable under Divine Law and shall not be subject to dissolution by any earthly authority.

Bill of Rights

The following rights are recognized as inherent to all living souls and are guaranteed to all members of the Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust:

Article I

The right to freedom of worship and religious practice without interference.

Article II

The right to due process in all ecclesiastical proceedings.

Article III

The right to privacy in personal affairs and ecclesiastical records.

Article IV

The right to own and hold property in allodial title.

Article V

The right to engage in lawful commerce free from unlawful interference.

Article VI

The right to travel freely upon the land and waters.

Article VII

The right to self-defense of person, family, and property.

Article VIII

The right to be free from unreasonable searches and seizures.

Article IX

The right to petition for redress of grievances.

Article X

All rights not expressly delegated are retained by the living soul.
