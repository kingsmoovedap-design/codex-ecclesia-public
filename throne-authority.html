Throne Authority

Powers & Responsibilities of Throne Holders

The Authority of the Thrones

Each of the Twelve Thrones carries specific divine authority and responsibility within the Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust. Throne holders are entrusted with sacred duties to serve the trust, protect its beneficiaries, and advance its purposes under divine law.

⚖️ Powers of Throne Holders

📜

Legislative Authority

Propose and vote on scrolls, decrees, and amendments to governance

⚖️

Judicial Authority

Serve on ecclesiastical courts and render judgments

🏛️

Executive Authority

Oversee ministry operations within their domain

🔖

Sealing Authority

Affix throne seal to official documents

👥

Appointment Authority

Appoint subordinate officers within their ministry

📋 Responsibilities

🛡️

Fiduciary Duty

Act in the best interest of the trust and its beneficiaries

📊

Reporting

Provide quarterly reports to the Council Chamber

🤝

Collaboration

Work with fellow throne holders for unified governance

📚

Education

Maintain expertise in their domain and train successors
