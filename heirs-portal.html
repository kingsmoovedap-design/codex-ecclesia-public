Frequently Asked Questions

What does it mean to be an Heir?

An Heir is a beneficiary of the Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust with inherent rights under Divine Law. As an Heir, you have beneficial interest in the trust and are protected by ecclesiastical law.

How do I claim my nationality?

Follow the Nation Claiming Roadmap above. Start with the Declaration of Status, then proceed through Heir Declaration, Naturalization, and Commercial Standing documents.

What is the difference between a living soul and a legal person?

A living soul is the flesh-and-blood man or woman created by God with unalienable rights. A legal person is a corporate fiction created by government through the birth certificate system, with only privileges granted by the state.

Is this recognized by the government?

The Ecclesia operates under 508(c)(1)(A) which is recognized in the Internal Revenue Code. Ecclesiastical organizations have immunity from many government regulations and maintain their own internal governance.

What documents do I need?

At minimum: Declaration of Status, Heir Declaration, and Certificate of Naturalization. For full commercial standing, also complete UCC-1 Financing Statement and Security Agreement.

How do I protect my property?

File a Land Patent or Allodial Title Claim for your property, along with a Homestead Declaration. These documents establish your superior claim under Divine Law rather than fee simple title.
