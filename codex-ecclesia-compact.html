Ecclesia Compact

Membership Agreement & Covenant

Preamble

This Ecclesia Compact establishes the sacred covenant between the Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust and its members. By entering into this compact, members affirm their commitment to the principles, purposes, and governance of the Ecclesia under divine law.

Article I: Purpose

The Ecclesia exists to provide a lawful, ecclesiastical organization for the benefit of its members, operating as a 508(c)(1)(A) organization under divine and natural law. The trust purposes include:

  • Preservation of sovereign rights and liberties
  • Education in divine law and lawful commerce
  • Protection of private property and assets
  • Establishment of ecclesiastical courts and governance
  • Creation of sovereign wealth and prosperity

Article II: Membership

Membership in the Ecclesia is voluntary and requires:

  • Affirmation of sovereign status under divine law
  • Agreement to abide by the Constitution and this Compact
  • Contribution to the trust treasury as specified
  • Participation in good faith in Ecclesia activities

Article III: Rights of Members

Members are entitled to:

  • Access to all public and private scrolls
  • Participation in assemblies and voting
  • Use of Ecclesia courts and dispute resolution
  • Protection under the trust umbrella
  • Inheritance and beneficiary rights as designated

Article IV: Obligations

Members agree to:

  • Honor the Constitution and divine law
  • Maintain confidentiality of private matters
  • Contribute to the common good of the Ecclesia
  • Respect fellow members and their rights
  • Support the trust purposes with integrity

Affirmation

By accepting this Compact, I affirm my sovereign standing under divine law and my voluntary entry into the Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust as a member in good standing.
