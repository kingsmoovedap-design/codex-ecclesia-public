⚖️ Legal Definitions

Ecclesiastical and legal terminology for Borders Ecclesia

Ecclesiastical Organization

A legally recognized religious body operating under 508(c)(1)(A) status, dedicated to sacred and charitable operations.

Grand Architect

The supreme executive officer and founder of Borders Ecclesia Earth Trust, responsible for strategic direction and final authority.

Trustees

Fiduciary agents responsible for administration, governance, and protection of organizational assets and heirs.

Sacred Scrolls

Digitally archived documents including constitutions, charters, deeds, and ecclesiastical records of the organization.

Dynasty

The integrated ecosystem of platforms including logistics, treasury, bail bonds, and Dynasty OS services.

Borders Sovereign Coin (BSC)

A utility token on Binance Smart Chain used for transactions, governance participation, and organizational operations.

Fair Market Value (FMV)

The estimated price at which an asset would sell between a willing buyer and willing seller, neither under pressure.

Escrow

A legal arrangement where a third party holds and regulates payment of funds required by two parties to a deal.

Blockchain Hash

A cryptographic fingerprint of a document that proves its integrity and immutability on the blockchain.

Filing

The formal recording of a document with the organization, creating an official record subject to blockchain verification.

Heir

A designated individual or entity with rights of succession to organizational assets, titles, or positions.

Burden of Proof

The obligation to prove assertions made in a legal proceeding, typically resting on the party making the claim.
