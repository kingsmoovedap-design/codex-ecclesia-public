Your Role as Trustee

🔐

Fiduciary Duty

As Trustee, you hold the highest duty of care, loyalty, and good faith toward the Trust and its beneficiaries. You must act solely in their best interest.

📋

Administration

Manage trust assets, maintain records, file documents, ensure compliance with ecclesiastical law, and protect the interests of all parties.

Legal Standing

You operate under 508(c)(1)(A) ecclesiastical immunity with full commercial standing in both private and public jurisdictions.
